Questions

Before you book

Do I need a paid Claude plan?

A free account is enough for sessions 1 to 3. For Projects, Claude Code and connectors, a Claude Pro plan (about USD 20 per month) is recommended during the cohort.

Is the certificate officially recognised?

It is an independent certificate, not an official Anthropic credential or an accredited diploma. Its value comes from what it proves: you built and defended a real workflow in front of an assessor. Each certificate has a verification ID so employers can check it.

What time are the sessions?

Sessions run on weekday evenings or Saturday mornings, Hong Kong / Singapore time (GMT+8). We pick the slot with the group before the first session.

Can I take the program in French?

Yes. French-speaking cohorts are available. Tell me your preferred language after booking.

What if I miss a session?

Every live session is recorded for your group. You can also join the same session in the next cohort once, at no cost.

Can my company pay?

Yes. You receive an invoice after purchase. For a private team cohort of 4, contact me before booking.

What if it is not for me?

If you attend Session 1 and feel the program is not right for you, ask for a full refund within 48 hours. No questions asked.

Is my data safe in the sessions?

You will learn what not to paste into any AI tool. In live sessions, we work on anonymised or non-confidential versions of your tasks.
